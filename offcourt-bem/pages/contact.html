Shipping & returns

FAQs.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 1–2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days. Orders $150+ ship free; otherwise shipping is $8.

What is your return policy?

Easy returns within 30 days, as long as the pair is unworn and in the original box. Start a return from the contact form below.

Are all pairs authentic?

Always. Every pair is sourced direct and checked before it leaves us. 100% authentic, guaranteed.

How do I find my size?

Use the size guide on this page. If you’re between sizes, we recommend going up half a size for running pairs.

Where is my order?

You’ll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. Can’t find it? Write to us and we’ll sort it.
